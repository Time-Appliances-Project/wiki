**Part of the Time Appliances Project (TAP) under the Open Compute Project**

## Objective

A collaborative space for driving innovation and coherence across hybrid multi-cloud infrastructure, AI systems, distributed architectures, time-aware computing, and emerging domains such as quantum and beyond.

## Overview

The Unified Intelligent Infrastructure (UII) workstream explores the convergence of AI, cloud-native platforms, data center infrastructure, and emerging compute paradigms. As modern workloads scale across hybrid and multi-cloud environments, the need for open, time-aware, intelligent infrastructure becomes increasingly critical.

This workstream serves as a platform to bring together community members, researchers, practitioners, and vendors to collaborate on challenges and innovations in:

- AI workload orchestration across distributed systems
- Intelligent data and control plane integration
- Time synchronization and temporal computing
- Infrastructure observability and automation
- Quantum, neuromorphic, and next-generation computing paradigms

## Deliverables

- Reference architectures and whitepapers
- Best practice guides and open design patterns
- Community presentations and speaker sessions
- Cross-domain collaboration with other TAP/OCP workstreams
- Experimental testbeds and PoC contributions
- Integration blueprints for time-aware and AI-ready platforms
