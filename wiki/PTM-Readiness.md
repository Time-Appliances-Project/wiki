## Precision Time Measurement Readiness Status

This list records hardware products and their reported Precision Time Measurement (PTM) support.

**Restored historical table:** All 35 entries below were recovered from the [original OCP wiki page archived on 3 August 2025](https://web.archive.org/web/20250803162843/https://www.opencompute.org/wiki/PTM_Readiness). That page was last edited on **1 July 2025** (revision 80269). Product labels, timing values, blank fields, and status wording are preserved from that snapshot; these are historical reports, not newly verified support claims.
